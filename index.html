Welcome to LCCWA

Driving Positive Change Together

LCCWA is a Mumbai-based non-profit organization dedicated to uplifting children from underprivileged communities in and around Powai. We work with students who attend overcrowded municipal and low-resourced private schools, where limited individual attention often affects learning outcomes.

"Every day, together with you, LCCWA works to drive positive change, enriching the lives of its students and empowering women to create a brighter future."

Our Core Mission

By providing supplementary academic support along with value-based education, we help bridge these gaps and nurture holistic development. Our mission is to enable children to learn more effectively, build confidence, develop strong values, and acquire the skills needed to create brighter, more independent futures for themselves and their communities.

Recent Events

LCCWA Sports Day 2025
21st December 2025

Celebrating Fitness, Fun, and Teamwork

On 21st December, LCCWA celebrated Sports Day with great enthusiasm. Supported by Bharat Cares under SMEC Trust and HDFC’s Parivartan Small Grants Programme, students took part in races, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, lemon and spoon race, and cricket.

Grade 11 and 12 volunteers helped organize the events. The cricket match was a highlight, with Grade 9 winning. Snacks and juice were served, and all participants received medals and trophies. We thank our sponsors for making the event successful.
